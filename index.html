How the model works

Thermal network. Each floor has an air node (air plus furniture) and an interior mass node (floors, partitions, party walls), linked by h·Am with Am = 3·floor area (ISO 13790 style). Exterior walls are a third node per floor, split into two equal resistances between sol-air temperature and indoor air. Floors couple through the floor/ceiling and the stair. Integrated explicitly at 60 s for 10 days; the first 7 are spin-up, the last 3 are shown.

Roof. Flat: a roof-deck node exchanges with the sol-air temperature outside and with the upstairs through the insulation. Pitched: deck node → attic air node (ventilated to outdoors) → ceiling insulation → upstairs.

Sun and sky. NOAA/Spencer solar position for the neighborhood's latitude and longitude (EST/EDT clock). Clear-sky GHI from Haurwitz, scaled so the daily total matches Philadelphia's monthly mean, then split into beam and diffuse with the Erbs correlation. Each façade and roof plane gets beam·cosθ + isotropic sky diffuse + ground reflection (albedo 0.2).

Street canyon. For the front façade, the opposite row (same height) casts a shadow line at Hb − Wstreet·tan(profile angle); window sunlit fraction and sky view factor (1 − sin ε)/2 follow from it.

Windows. Transmitted solar = glazed area (85%) × SHGC × incidence-angle modifier × blind factor; 30% goes to air, 70% to the mass. Conduction U·A to outdoor air. Internal gains follow a residential schedule.

Opaque surfaces. Sol-air temperature To + α·I/ho − εΔR/ho (ho = 17 W/m²K, ΔR = 63 W/m² for a horizontal surface, scaled by cloudiness). Basement/ground follows an annual sinusoid around 13.5 °C.

Weather. Hourly air temperature from 1991–2020 PHL monthly mean max/min with the ASHRAE diurnal profile, plus the neighborhood's UHI offset (day value at noon, night value after dark). Heat waves add the chosen ΔT on three clear days.

Limits. Single typical day per month, no wind-driven or stack-driven flow model, no detailed longwave exchange between rooms. The neighborhood UHI values are an illustrative proxy, not measurements; import measured offsets for real analysis.
